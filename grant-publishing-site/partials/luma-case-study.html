A cover and interior.

A coordinated cover redesign and interior formatting for John Capon’s children’s picture book, Luma the Sleepy Star. The project brought the front and back cover together, introduced child-friendly typography, and turned the supplied manuscript into a 35-page print interior with ownership and title pages.
