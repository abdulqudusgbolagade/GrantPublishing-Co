Amazon Book Visibility

Help the right reader recognise where your book belongs.

THE PURPOSE

What this service is for

A published book can be available without presenting a clear reason for its intended reader to choose it. This service reviews how your Amazon listing communicates its subject, genre and reader appeal.

What we can work on

  • Review of the public book listing and its positioning
  • Recommendations for relevant keywords and category fit
  • Checks for consistent author, series and edition information
  • A prioritised improvement plan with agreed implementation support

What to share

Share your Amazon link, intended reader and current concern. For paid work, a synopsis or manuscript helps us check that recommendations match the book itself.
