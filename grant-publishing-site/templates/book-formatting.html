THE PURPOSE

What this service is for

Turn your manuscript into a readable book interior. We plan the layout for your chosen formats, so typography, spacing, chapter openings and front matter work together.

What we can work on

  • Ebook and Kindle formatting with navigation and a table of contents appropriate to the content
  • Paperback and hardcover interior layouts at the agreed trim size
  • Front matter, chapter styles, page numbering and image placement
  • Print-ready interior PDFs and ebook-ready files in the agreed formats
  • KDP-ready file preparation, agreed revisions and a clear handover

Need the cover too? Explore book cover design.

What to share

Share your manuscript, word count, illustrations or tables, chosen formats and publication plans. We will confirm file types, trim size and any special layout requirements. Editing and new illustrations are separate services.
