The challenge and starting materials

The project began with an existing cover direction, a supplied 33-page layout/manuscript and established story illustrations. The task was to bring those materials into a consistent cover and interior presentation, rather than create a new story or replace its illustrations.

The work needed to consider title and author hierarchy, front-to-back cover continuity, a readable back-cover copy area and a comfortable reading layout inside the book.

Design and formatting decisions

The cover redesign connected the front and back through the book’s Sky Realm imagery. The title, character and author name were given a coordinated hierarchy. Back-cover copy was placed in a defined area that could be read against the artwork.

Inside the book, child-friendly typography, compact read-aloud lines and attention to the gutter supported the supplied text and illustrations. Ownership and title pages introduced the book before the story. Alternating text and illustration pages kept the visual presentation consistent.

Work completed and the final print package

  • A full paperback cover wrap with coordinated front, spine and back-cover presentation.
  • A 35-page formatted interior, including ownership and title pages.
  • Final cover and interior print PDFs, with embedded fonts in the interior.
  • Bleed-ready interior pages measuring 8.625 × 8.75 inches for the agreed 8.5 × 8.5-inch trim.

Delivered outcomes and the boundary of the work

The observable outcome was a coordinated paperback cover and formatted interior, delivered on 8 October 2026. The supplied 33-page layout/manuscript became a 35-page print interior with the added front matter. The final PDFs brought the cover and reading layout together as a print package.

This project ended at file delivery. It did not include KDP upload, publication, metadata optimisation or Kindle conversion. Amazon KDP was the intended platform, not evidence of a completed upload or platform approval.

The story illustrations were supplied. No sales, ranking or advertising results are claimed, and the design decisions are not presented as a measured increase in readability or commercial performance.

John Capon’s existing review is preserved on Client Feedback.
