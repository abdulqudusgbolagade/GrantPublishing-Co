Series & Catalog Strategy

Think beyond a single book.

THE PURPOSE

What this service is for

A reader who enjoys one title should be able to understand what to read next. We examine how your books connect, where their presentation differs and which title provides a useful starting point.

What we can work on

  • Catalog overview and reader pathway review
  • Author, series and edition consistency checks
  • Recommendations for book-to-book connections
  • A prioritised plan for strengthening the catalog

What to share

Share the links to your books, their reading order and any upcoming releases. A synopsis for each title helps us avoid assuming that every book serves the same reader.
