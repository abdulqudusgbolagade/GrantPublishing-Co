THE PURPOSE

What this service is for

Give your book a considered visual presentation. We work on the cover concept, typography and layout, with the genre, reader and edition specifications in view.

What we can work on

  • Cover direction and ebook front cover design
  • Paperback or hardcover cover layout within the agreed specifications
  • Title, author name, spine and back cover presentation
  • Final cover files and agreed revisions

What to share

Share your title, author name, genre, synopsis, references and any artwork you already have. Print covers need confirmed trim size, paper specifications and page count. Illustration creation and image licensing are included only when agreed.
