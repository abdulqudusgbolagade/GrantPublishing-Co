THE PURPOSE

What this service is for

Book marketing works alongside a well-presented publication. Supporting services include formatting, cover design and publishing consultation, with the deliverables matched to your book and chosen formats.

What we can work on

  • Kindle and print formatting within the agreed specifications
  • Cover design or cover presentation support
  • Publishing preparation and file review
  • Clear deliverables, revisions and handover requirements

What to share

Tell us your book type, word count, desired formats and target publication date. Include existing files where relevant. Editing, illustration, ISBNs and platform fees are included only if explicitly agreed.
